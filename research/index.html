Linear does not mean regular: Jacobian rank limits the learning coefficient
2026
Tested whether a network that is linear in its parameters looks regular under the local learning coefficient. It does not: the Jacobian rank is capped by the data rather than the parameter count, so the NTK-scaled network's effective dimension is nearly width-invariant, while the muP-scaled one falls with width and ends up about 4x more degenerate at width 1024. Identifies the finite-damping estimate as the ridge effective degrees of freedom, which explains why a fixed damping scale reversed the ordering, and replaces the sampler with a Hessian-based reference computation that removes the scale dependence.
